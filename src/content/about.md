### Faces in stone, figures in light.

The name comes from pareidolia — the quiet habit of finding a presence in things that have none. A figure in rusted
iron, a watchful gull on ancient stone, a window that seems to breathe.

These photographs were gathered slowly: along temple paths after the rain, beneath vaulted ceilings, at the edge of
still water. Nothing is staged. Each frame is simply a moment that asked to be kept.
